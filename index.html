Good to know
before you go.

IN A PINCH maps restroom stops across the United States. Choose a state on the map, search for a town or open one of the 24 illustrated city guides.

Reading the atlas

Each facility type has a color and symbol. The Map key identifies them. Numbered groups show stops in a wider area. Zoom in to see individual places. Neighborhood searches load community-mapped restrooms, gas stations, rest areas, restaurants, bars and other places with possible bathroom access. Some areas have few mapped stops.

Checking access

“Mapped bathrooms” shows places with restroom evidence. The Showers filter shows stops where a source documents a shower. Check opening hours and fees with the operator. At venues marked “Bathroom unconfirmed,” ask staff about access. Pins may point to a building or park entrance. Directions open in Google Maps.

At a hotel

Ask the front desk about lobby restrooms. Private guest-room showers generally require a booking. Hotel cards distinguish dining listed by the operator, dining mapped within the hotel footprint, and places nearby.

Reading the hours

“Open by schedule” uses the posted restroom hours in the facility’s time zone. Access and hours can change.

Finding nearby stops

Allow location access to center on nearby stops. You can also pan or choose a destination. Your location centers the map. Saved stops stay in this browser.

Your ratings

Rate a restroom you used, then sort by your highest-rated stops. Ratings stay in this browser on this device. You can change or clear them in the listing.
